University Academic Policy Handbook

Course Registration:
Students must register for courses within the first two weeks of each semester.
Late registration is allowed only with department head approval and a late fee of Rs. 500.

Attendance Policy:
Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory to be eligible for final examinations.
Students with attendance between 65-75% may apply for condonation through the Dean's office.

Grading System:
The university follows a 10-point CGPA system.
A grade below 4.0 CGPA in any semester requires the student to repeat that semester.

Exam Deadlines:
Mid-semester exams are held in the 8th week of each semester.
End-semester exams begin two weeks after the last teaching day.

Fee Payment:
Tuition fees must be paid before the start of each semester.
A late fee of 2% per week applies after the due date.

Leave of Absence:
Students may apply for a leave of absence for up to one semester with valid documentation.
Medical leave requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.
